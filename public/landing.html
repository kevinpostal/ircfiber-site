FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

If yours isn't here, ask in #ircfiber @ libera or email team@ircfiber.com.

Where are my messages stored?
On our infrastructure. We run a multi-region edge fleet (US-East, EU-West, AP-South) backed by encrypted-at-rest storage. You pick the region at signup; we don't move your data between regions without telling you. We never sell, share, or mine your messages — they're yours, full stop.
Do I need to install anything?
No. The web app runs in any modern browser. No native client, no Electron wrapper, no npm install. You can install it as a PWA to your home screen for a more app-like feel, but that's optional.
What if I already use a desktop client like WeeChat or HexChat?
Keep using it. IRC Fiber's gateway exposes a standard bouncer protocol, so you can point WeeChat, HexChat, irssi, mIRC, or anything else at it and get the same always-on buffer state. The web app is the convenience layer; you're not locked into it.
Can I add my own private IRC network?
Yes. The network form takes hostname, port, password, and SASL credentials. The bouncer maintains the connection just like the public networks. 100+ public networks are preconfigured (Libera, OFTC, hackint, EFnet, QuakeNet, and more).
What happens if I cancel?
You get a 30-day grace period to come back, then we delete your account and message history. We also offer a one-click export of every buffer as plain text or JSON, so you can take your scrollback with you if you decide to self-host something else.
