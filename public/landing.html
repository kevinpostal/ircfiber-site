Features

Everything you'd expect from a serious IRC client.

The same IRC you already know — the bouncer, the slash commands, the mode prefixes, the formatting — running in a browser, on our infrastructure, synced to every device you sign in from.

Always connected

Our edge fleet of bouncers stays in the channel 24/7. Power off your laptop, take a flight, lose wifi — when you sign back in, every message is waiting.

Full history

Every message, in every buffer, stored in our cloud. Scroll back through a week of conversation or a year — same scrollback, same slash commands.

Any device

Modern web app that works in every browser, on every device. Open it on your phone, switch to a desktop, jump to a tablet — the same buffers follow you.

responsivepwa-ready

Multi-network

100+ IRC networks preconfigured — Libera, OFTC, hackint, EFnet, QuakeNet, and more. Add your own private network with one form.
